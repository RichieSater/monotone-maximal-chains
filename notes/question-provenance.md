# Provenance of the question

Victor S. Monakhov communicated the universal-existence question to Richie
Sater by email on August 8, 2026, and attributed the proposal jointly to
himself and Irina L. Sokhor. The manuscript states an equivalent, polished
formulation of the question.

This public note records only the attribution and date needed to document the
origin of the problem. It deliberately does not reproduce private
correspondence.

Monakhov and Sokhor's 2025 article, *On Indices of Maximal Chains in Finite
Groups*, calls a maximal chain with nondecreasing successive indices a
`(<)`-chain. The article is cited for this terminology and the surrounding
background, not as a published source of the universal-existence question.
