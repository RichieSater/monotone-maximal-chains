# Provenance of the question

Correspondence among Victor S. Monakhov, Irina L. Sokhor, and Richie Sater
beginning on August 8, 2026, brought the universal-existence question to
Sater's attention. The manuscript states an equivalent, polished formulation
of the question.

This public note records only the participants and date needed to document the
transmission of the problem. It deliberately does not reproduce private
correspondence and makes no claim about the problem's original formulation or
priority.

Monakhov and Sokhor's 2025 article, *On Indices of Maximal Chains in Finite
Groups*, calls a maximal chain with nondecreasing successive indices a
`(<)`-chain. The article is cited for this terminology and the surrounding
background, not as a published source of the universal-existence question.
